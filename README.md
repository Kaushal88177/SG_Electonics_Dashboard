# 📊 SG Collections - Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI Sales Dashboard for **SG Electronics (SG Collections)** to track sales, profit, and customer performance across countries, categories, and brands.

### 🔗 Live Dashboard Preview

---

### 📌 Overview
This dashboard provides a 360° view of the business. It helps management to understand:
- Overall business growth
- Seasonality and month-wise trends
- Which country, category and brand is driving revenue
- Sales vs Profit comparison

### 🎯 Key KPIs
| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Orders** | 12K |
| **Total Sales** | 9.48M |
| **Total Profit** | 5.85M |
| **Profit Margin** | ~61.7% |

### 📈 Visuals in this Dashboard

**1. Month Wise Transactions (Line Chart)**
- Tracks monthly sales trend.
- Peak Sales in Feb (1.25M) and Dec (Highest).
- Huge drop in April (92.9K) - needs investigation.

**2. Store Country Wise Sales (Pie Chart)**
- United States is the top performing store country with 1,965.34K (20.72%) of total sales.
- Followed by other countries like United Kingdom, Canada, Germany.

**3. Sales by Category (Donut Chart)**
- **Computers** is the highest selling category - 609K (20.24%)
- Followed by Cell Phones - 353K (11.71%)
- Other categories: Home Appliances, TV and Video, Cameras, Audio, Music & Movies, Games and Toys.

**4. Sales & Profit by Brands (Clustered Column Chart)**
- **Adventure Works** leads in both Sales (2007.65K) and Profit (1320.67K)
- Top 3 Brands: Adventure Works, Wide World Importers (1673.93K), Contoso (1814.27K)
- Low performing brands: Tailspin Toys (125.11K) - opportunity to discontinue or re-market.

**5. Sales by Customer Country (Treemap)**
- **United States** customers generate highest revenue - 4,669.03K (~49% of total)
- 2nd is United Kingdom - 1,198.35K
- 3rd is Canada - 903.77K

### 💡 Key Insights & Business Recommendations

1.  **US Dominance:** Both store and customer base is heavily US-centric. Focus expansion in UK & Germany where potential is high.
2.  **Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct-Dec) shows consistent growth - perfect for holiday campaigns. April slump needs root cause analysis.
3.  **Category Focus:** Computers & Cell Phones are 30%+ of business. Stock and marketing should prioritize them.
4.  **Brand Strategy:** Adventure Works & Contoso are cash cows. Invest more in their marketing.
5.  **Profitability:** Overall profit margin is very healthy at 61.7%.

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Tool:** Power BI Desktop

- **Features Used:** Interactive Slicers (Store, Year, Month), Drill-down, Custom Dark Theme
- **Data Cleaning:** Power Query
